a letter that matters to me

hey, bella.

i've been trying to find the right words, and i think the hardest part is knowing that words on their own are not enough.

you are such a special person. not because you're always smiling or because everything about you is easy. it's the way you care, the way you feel things deeply, and the way you're still yourself even on the days when the world feels heavy. there's so much about you that's worth protecting.

and i know i've let you down. i'm not going to hide that behind a pretty website or ask you to forget how you feel. you deserved better from me, and i'm sorry for the hurt i caused.

i wish i could make you see how genuinely important you are to me. not by asking you to believe a paragraph, but by becoming someone whose actions are more thoughtful, honest, and careful with your trust.

you don't owe me a reply or forgiveness. i just wanted you to have something that says, in a way i sometimes struggle to say out loud: i see you, i care about you, and i'm grateful that i've gotten to know you.

however you feel right now, i respect it. and whatever happens next, please don't let my mistakes make you feel like you're anything less than someone wonderful.

you deserve kindness that feels safe. always.

with care,
andrew ♡
